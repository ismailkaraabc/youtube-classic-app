Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

youtube-classic analytics is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels it reads. It requests read-only access (YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data API: youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly) to the owner's own channels.

What is accessed

Channel and video statistics: views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, audience retention, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate, and video titles.

How it is used and stored

The data is used only to help the owner judge and improve their own videos. Access tokens and reports are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data of other YouTube users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
